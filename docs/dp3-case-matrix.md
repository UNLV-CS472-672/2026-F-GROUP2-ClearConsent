## What complete coverage means

Every committed FR-1–FR-12 behavior has a case and an outcome. Critical paths have deterministic automated checks at the lowest useful level, including failure and ownership cases. An integrated test proves a newly submitted source, its analysis, and the reopened result refer to the **same content**. Controlled live checks and a participant study supply evidence mocks cannot. Line/branch percentages are reported separately; 100% line coverage alone does not establish a working product.

`Unit` means Vitest with local fixtures/mocked providers. `DB` means disposable Supabase with synthetic rows. `Browser` means Playwright against a deterministic test environment. `Live` means a bounded manual check outside CI. These lanes are not interchangeable.

The historical `5d182f3` Vitest baseline was **55.57% lines (843/1,517)** and **61.83% branches (559/904)** over production TypeScript and Svelte. [#89](https://github.com/UNLV-CS472-672/2026-F-GROUP2-ClearConsent/pull/89) added the CI coverage gate; [#91](https://github.com/UNLV-CS472-672/2026-F-GROUP2-ClearConsent/pull/91) raised its floors to 59% lines and 65% branches. Current remote `main` CI reports **74.19% lines, 77.07% branches, and 579 passing unit tests**. It also passed four anonymous and five authenticated browser cases, none of which is the ordinary content-linked E1 journey. Record final percentages and covered/total counts at the final commit; browser, DB, and offline suites remain separate.
